Nyla remembers what happened before 3:17. But remembering the truth may be the thing that finally lets it remember her.

THIS IS THE LAST RESET
3:16 AM · BEFORE THE RESET

Nyla opened her eyes.

She was back in the library.

Not the hallway.

Not the apartment.

The library.

Three months earlier.

A red backpack lay beside a chair.

Cheap headphones rested on a table.

Someone was crying behind the locked door.

Nyla remembered now.

Her name was Mira.

MEMORY RECOVERED
MIRA

Mira had been trapped inside the library's old archive room.

She had heard Nyla's voice outside.

But it wasn't Nyla.

Something had copied her voice.

MIRA

“Nyla? Please open the door.”

Nyla had opened it.

That was the first mistake.

Aanya had screamed.

The lights had gone out.

And something had stepped through the doorway.

Not a person.

Not exactly.

The door had slammed shut.

Mira had disappeared.

Nyla had spent the next three months trying to remember what happened.

But every time she got close...

3:17 happened.

SHE HADN'T BEEN TRAPPED IN THE LOOP.

She had created it.

Nyla closed her eyes.

“I'm sorry.”

A voice answered from behind her.

MIRA

“You said that before.”

Nyla turned.

Mira stood beside the archive door.

Red backpack.

Cheap headphones.

Same face from the photograph.

But her eyes were completely black.

MIRA

“You promised you wouldn't leave me here.”

Nyla couldn't breathe.

“I came back.”

MIRA

“No.”

“You came back because you wanted to save yourself.”

Nyla looked at the clock.

3:16.

One minute remained.

She finally understood.

The loop wasn't asking her to open the door.

It wasn't asking her to close it.

It was asking her to choose.

Choose Mira.

Choose Aanya.

Choose herself.

Every previous Nyla had chosen someone.

And every choice created another version.

Nyla stared at the message.

Then she smiled.

For the first time that night, she wasn't afraid.

NYLA

“No.”

MIRA

“What?”

NYLA

The clock changed.

3:17

Nyla picked up her phone.

She typed three names.

MEMORY
I remember Mira.
I remember Aanya.
I remember myself.

The phone screen cracked.

Not physically.

The image on it cracked.

Like reality was glass.

Mira stepped backward.

MIRA

“What are you doing?”

NYLA

“Remembering all of it.”

The hallway appeared.

Then the apartment.

Then the library.

All three places existed at once.

Every Nyla appeared.

Every Aanya.

Every version of Mira.

Hundreds of them.

All staring at her.

“I'M NOT CHOOSING.”

Nyla said it louder.

“I remember all of you.”

The clocks stopped.

3:17.

3:17.

3:17.

Then one clock moved.

3:18

The first minute after the loop.

THE RESET FAILED.

Everything disappeared.

The hallway.

The doors.

The copies.

Mira.

Even the sound of breathing.

Nyla opened her eyes.

Morning.

Sunlight.

Her bedroom.

Her phone beside her.

7:42 AM.

She sat up.

There was no knocking.

No shadow.

No mysterious account.

She almost laughed.

Then her phone vibrated.

AANYA · 7:43 AM
you awake?

Nyla stared at the screen.

She typed:

I remember Mira.

The typing indicator appeared.

Three dots.

They disappeared.

Then appeared again.

Then disappeared.

Aanya finally replied:

Who is Mira?

Nyla stopped breathing.

Her phone vibrated again.

UNKNOWN ACCOUNT
good
you remembered
now don't look behind you

Nyla slowly looked at the mirror.

REFLECTION DETECTED

Her reflection was there.

So was Aanya's reflection.

And Mira's.

Three girls.

Nyla turned around.

There was no one behind her.

She looked back at the mirror.

The three reflections were still there.

Only two of them were breathing.

THE STORY IS OVER

THE END

3:17 ended.

The night moved forward.

Nyla remembered.

Mira was no longer forgotten.

And for the first time, there was no second knock.
