THE STORY

Something is wrong.

It started with a phone that unlocked itself. Then came the messages. Then the photographs. And eventually, Nyla realized someone was using her identity better than she was.

Nyla is twenty, permanently online, and absolutely convinced she knows her own life.

Until one night, at exactly 3:17 AM, her phone receives a message from an account that shouldn't exist.

The username is hers. The profile picture is hers. And whoever is behind it knows what happens next.

THE FIRST RULE

If your own account messages you... don't answer.
