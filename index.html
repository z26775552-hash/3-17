Nyla is twenty, in college, always online and usually one notification away from being distracted.

But one night her phone unlocks at exactly 3:16 AM.

A message appears.

The problem is... she doesn't remember taking them.

ABOUT 3:17

3:17 is a psychological horror story built around identity, memory and the strange things that can happen when our digital lives begin behaving like they have a mind of their own.

The story follows Nyla as she tries to understand why her own digital identity seems to know more about her life than she does.

But the deeper she goes, the less certain she becomes about one question:

Who is actually Nyla?
