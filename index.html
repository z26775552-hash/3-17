01 — THE STORY

Some things are better left unopened.

Nyla is twenty, in college, always online, always connected, and usually one notification away from being distracted.

But one night, at exactly 3:16 AM, her phone unlocks by itself.

Then a message arrives from an account that should belong to her.

Except Nyla didn't send it.

THE FIRST RULE
Don't answer it.
Whatever happens at 3:17, don't answer the message. Don't look behind you. And most importantly, don't trust what your phone shows you.
